**Verdict:** The YouTube library is clean and understandable, but small type and dimmed unfocused cards keep it from being comfortably 10-foot/D-pad ready.

### Problems and fixes
1. **Readability:** “2 videos,” “DAVE GRAY (1),” card titles, and “7:21” appear only ~12–16 logical px at 1280×720; rail labels are similarly small. Use 22 px card titles with 28 px line height (two lines max), 18 px durations/counts, 20 px group headings and rail labels; leave 8 px between title and duration.
2. **Focus versus legibility:** The purple outline around “This Changes How You Should Think About AI” is clear, but “Everything Has Changed!” and its still are excessively dimmed, obscuring the next destination. Keep unfocused cards at 100% opacity; use a 4 px pale focus ring with 4 px offset and reserve 8 px around each card to prevent clipping.
3. **Label wording:** “YouTube  2 videos,” “UNCATEGORIZED (1),” and “DAVE GRAY (1)” are inconsistent and overly technical. Use “YouTube · 2 videos,” “Uncategorized · 1 video,” and “Dave Gray · 1 video”; place counts 12 px after headings and keep 16 px between each heading and its row.

### Priorities
- **P0:** Increase TV typography to the sizes above; verify titles and durations from seating distance.
- **P1:** Remove unfocused dimming and retain the stronger, clipped-proof focus ring.
- **P2:** Normalize channel/count wording and spacing for faster scanning.
