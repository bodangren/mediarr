# YouTube TV — Fullscreen Playback (MiniMax M3.1 Flash review)

**Verdict:** Clean, cinematic full-bleed playback — but with the overlay hidden, a 10-foot viewer learns nothing about what is playing, how much is left, or how to get controls; the remote carries everything and the screen never teaches it.

**1 — No context at rest.** Title, channel, progress and remaining time are invisible; from the couch you cannot tell episode 3 of 12 from a 4-minute clip.
*Fix:* Permanent bottom chrome — scrim bar with channel + title, progress bar, elapsed/remaining time. Bottom placement dodges the creator's burned-in inset graphic up top.

**2 — Hidden overlay, no discoverability.** Nothing hints that Center/OK opens controls; no guaranteed reveal, so a still frame reads as a frozen app.
*Fix:* Auto-show the transport overlay for 5s on entry with an "OK · Back" hint row; any D-pad press reveals it instantly (8s timeout); keep pause/seek/speed remote-only, and put captions/speed/audio on the remote's long-press menu.

**3 — Invisible exit path.** Nothing signals that Back returns to the library, so viewers can drop out mid-episode.
*Fix:* "Back to <series>" hint for 10s; past 30s elapsed, Back opens a resume/exit confirm sheet.

**Priorities**
- **P0:** Auto-reveal the transport overlay ~5s after entry with a visible "OK opens controls" hint — the only teachable path to playback.
- **P1:** Persistent title, channel, progress bar and elapsed/remaining time while the overlay is hidden.
- **P2:** "Back to library" hint, plus a confirm-before-exit guard after 30s.
