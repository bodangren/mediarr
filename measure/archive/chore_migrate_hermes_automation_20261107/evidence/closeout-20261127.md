## 2. Defect found by the window and fixed: one cadence, one threshold

`subtitle-wanted-priority` runs **daily at 06:30**, but `checkSchedulerTasks` applied the global
**180-minute** staleness limit to every expected task, so the watchdog raised a critical
`WATCHDOG_SCHEDULER_TASKS` alert for ~21 hours of every day — **156 criticals in the window**
(72 on 11-27 alone) and the cause of 174 pending alerts. Fixed TDD in **`41786b90`**:
`checkSchedulerTasks` honours `scheduler.maxTaskStalenessMinutesByTask`; `expectations.yaml` gives
`subtitle-wanted-priority: 1560` (26 h = one missed daily run, the same reasoning as
`maxHeartbeatAgeMinutes`). Plugin suite **25/25**; the live plugin was reloaded (disable/enable,
`include:media-automation`) and the watchdog now returns
`ok:true, "scheduler-tasks: 4 expected tasks fresh"` (2026-11-27T13:32Z).
